# Five concepts: creative selection, not simulated judge scores

Research snapshot: November 16, 2026, 325 entries on the official development
leaderboard. Read every entry title, retained linked coin URLs, and inspected
official image previews for the leading five entries. Scores/ranks are
provisional; development scores are against house anchors, not a final contest
between all entrants. Titles show advertised themes, not every full description.
Individual pump.fun pages were inaccessible through the web tool.

Source: https://make.fun/api/competitions/makefun-coin-promo-01/leaderboard
Raw snapshots: evidence/leaderboard-current.json and evidence/field-current.json.
Official preview images: evidence/competitor-1.webp through competitor-5.webp.

## Crowded themes and underrepresented opportunities

Crowded: first jobs, paychecks and invoices; ghost/hound/bot bounty hunters;
escrow vaults, certificates and legal verdicts; generic ship/proof/payday claims.
Leading previews showed FINAL forms, a make.fun prize cheque, a nervous robot
mugshot, NO HUMAN packaging, and a wanted poster. Each conveys one clear joke.

Less visible in the title scan: playful problem transformation, merit without
CV theatre, skill-training rituals, human/agent relay, and test-kitchen
experimentation. No title matched pinata, shredder/resume, gym, chef/kitchen,
relay or baton. This does not prove absence from metadata, images or later entries.

## Selection: Problem Pinata / SOLVEPOP

Creativity is the official judge's first criterion. BOTPAY repeated who got paid.
Problem Pinata makes the challenge itself the mascot: the prize is inside the
problem. The code-shaped opening, trophy, cursor-stick, name and description
tell one story. make.fun supplies the action behind it: find a bounty, build
software with an agent and compete for cash rewards.

It balances clearer prize motivation than the gym, fewer visual parts than the
relay, less paperwork overlap than the shredder and a more direct challenge/prize
connection than the kitchen. No model-assigned scores were used to select it.

Risks: the seven-point pinata can read as a party star; the trophy and code
brackets help, but do not make the metaphor universal. Generic pinata coins and
the Pinata storage company exist. The coined SOLVEPOP ticker trades familiarity
for differentiation. No trademark clearance, buyer demand or winning placement
is guaranteed. This is a stronger first-place-oriented candidate, not a prediction.
