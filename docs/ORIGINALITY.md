## Conclusion

Independently authored artwork and implementation: supported by native evidence.
Distinctive enough to be called a new competitive narrative: **not established**.
The employment/receipt theme is crowded and BOTPAY has naming collisions.
The package is a coherent development candidate, but a high-confidence
originality claim or predicted winning placement would be unjustified.
Before launch approval, recommend a materially different story and a newly
screened ticker, or explicit acceptance of these limitations. No rebranding,
launch or submission has been performed on the user's behalf.
